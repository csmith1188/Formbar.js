# Items and Inventory

## Obtaining Items

Items are obtained through exchanges and pool creation. When you create a pool, 100 shares (by default) will be added to your inventory. These shares can be exchanged with other users to give them some ownership of a pool. Pool payouts are based on the amount of shares you own in a pool. 

For more information on exchanges, visit the [Exchanges Page](https://github.com/csmith1188/Formbar.js/wiki/Exchanges).
For more information on pool payouts and pool creation, visit the [Digipogs Page](https://github.com/csmith1188/Formbar.js/wiki/Digipogs)

## Viewing your inventory.

You can view your inventory items by going to your profile and clicking on the `Inventory` button. Here will be displayed all the shares you own and the name of each pool. 

From this menu, you can view all the items you own, and remove them from your inventory. To remove them, click on the share item, select an amount you want to remove, and click the `Delete` button. This will remove the specified amount of shares from your inventory and give them to the highest shareholder. If you are the highest share holder, nothing happens.
